## 3. Symlink

All data we are going to need are under `/home/ubuntu/Share/genome_assembly`. But you won't execute anything from there. We are copying files over or making symbolic links to the folders you just created in your home directory.

Make a [symbolic link](https://www.futurelearn.com/info/courses/linux-for-bioinformatics/0/steps/201767#:~:text=A%20symlink%20is%20a%20symbolic,directory%20in%20any%20file%20system.) of scripts to your folder. Do as below:

```
# go inside your folder
cd genome_assembly_day1

#symlink the two scripts
ln -s /home/ubuntu/Share/genome_assembly/asmstats .
ln -s /home/ubuntu/Share/genome_assembly/plot_read_length_dist.py .
```
